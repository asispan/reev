# Reev — Play Store listing

## App name (≤30)
Reev: Private Voice Keyboard

## Short description (≤80)
Hold to talk, let go and it's typed. On-device dictation in your keyboard.

## Full description
Reev is a keyboard with a voice mode built in. Hold the mic, talk, let go, and your words appear wherever you're typing. It works in any app, and it never draws over other apps.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• On-device speech recognition. NVIDIA Parakeet runs on your phone, so your voice never leaves it. Works offline.
• No account, no analytics, no ads, no servers of ours.
• Lives only inside the keyboard. Reev never asks for "display over other apps", so it doesn't trip the overlay protections in banking and payment apps.

YOUR ENGINE, YOUR CHOICE
Prefer a cloud model? Add your own ElevenLabs, Cartesia, Smallest AI or Sarvam API key. Your key stays on your phone, and audio goes straight from your phone to the provider you picked. Cloud is off unless you turn it on.

A REAL KEYBOARD, NOT JUST A MIC
• Glide typing: swipe across letters to type a word.
• Smart suggestions that read context: "we'll" vs "well", "it's" vs "its".
• Next-word predictions.
• Hinglish support: kya, nahi, yaar are words, not typos.
• Every emoji, with recents.
• Light, dark, or follow the system.
• One-tap switch to your other keyboards.

CLEAN DICTATION
Filler words like "um" and "uh" are removed, and capitalisation and punctuation are tidied.

PERMISSIONS
• Microphone: only while you hold the mic button.
• Internet: to download the on-device model once, and for cloud engines if you choose one.

Open source: github.com/asispan/reev
